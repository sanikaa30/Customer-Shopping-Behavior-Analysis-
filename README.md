# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to identify meaningful patterns in customer purchasing, spending habits, product preferences, discounts, subscriptions, and seasonal trends.

The analysis combines **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to perform data analysis and create an interactive dashboard for better business insights and decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Identify revenue patterns across different customer segments
* Compare spending behavior based on gender and age groups
* Analyze the impact of discounts on customer purchases
* Understand subscriber vs. non-subscriber spending behavior
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Analyze seasonal sales performance
* Explore customer preferences for payment and shipping methods

## 🔍 SQL Analysis

The project includes several SQL queries to answer important business questions, including:

### 💰 Revenue Analysis

* Total revenue generated by male and female customers
* Revenue contribution by different age groups
* Revenue performance by product category
* Product revenue ranking

### 👥 Customer Analysis

* Customer segmentation into **New, Returning, and Loyal** customers
* Spending comparison between subscribers and non-subscribers
* Analysis of repeat buyers and subscription behavior
* Identification of high-spending age groups

### 🛍️ Product Analysis

* Top-rated products based on customer reviews
* Top purchased products within each category
* Product ranking based on revenue performance

### 🎁 Discount Analysis

* Customers who used discounts but spent above the average purchase amount
* Products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases

### 📦 Other Business Insights

* Comparison between Standard and Express shipping
* Seasonal sales performance
* Customer spending behavior by payment method
